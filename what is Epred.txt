# EPred

**EPred (Energy Predictor )** is a machine-learning-based project focused on forecasting electrical energy consumption using historical energy measurements and environmental parameters.

The project aims to analyse electricity consumption patterns, understand the influence of environmental factors on energy usage, and develop predictive models capable of estimating future appliance energy consumption.

Using real-world energy consumption data, EPred applies data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering and supervised machine learning techniques to develop and evaluate regression models.

The initial implementation focuses on comparing Linear Regression, Decision Tree Regression, Random Forest Regression and Gradient Boosting Regression to identify an effective predictive model.

The long-term vision of EPred is to evolve into an intelligent energy management system capable of real-time energy monitoring, consumption forecasting, abnormal usage detection and energy optimization.

**Primary Objective:** To develop a reliable machine learning model that predicts electrical energy consumption and provides insights into energy usage behaviour.

**Initial Scope:** Dataset analysis, preprocessing, regression model development, prediction and performance evaluation.

**Future Scope:** Integration with IoT sensors, real-time monitoring, energy optimization and intelligent energy management.
